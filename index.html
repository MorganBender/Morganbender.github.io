WHO I AM

ITHACA COLLEGE / PARK SCHOOL

About
me.

I'm Morgan Bender, a media student at Ithaca College's Park School of Communications. I'm interested in film, sports media, and the feeling you get when a story lands just right.

I've filmed lacrosse, cut highlight reels, and learned to find a story in the real moments. I love being behind a camera, shaping a scene in the edit, and finding the music that brings it all together. I'm still learning, still experimenting, and always looking for a more thoughtful way to tell the story.
